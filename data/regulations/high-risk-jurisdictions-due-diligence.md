docId: high-risk-jurisdictions-due-diligence
title: Enhanced due diligence for high-risk jurisdictions and correspondent accounts
citation: 31 CFR 1010.610
source: FinCEN / Bank Secrecy Act
---

A covered financial institution must apply enhanced due diligence to correspondent accounts
established or maintained for foreign financial institutions that operate under banking licenses
in jurisdictions designated as non-cooperative with international AML principles, or that have
been the subject of special measures. Enhanced due diligence is risk-based and scales with the
money-laundering risk presented by the foreign institution and its home jurisdiction.

Transactions involving high-risk geographies — including jurisdictions identified by the
Financial Action Task Force (FATF) as having strategic AML/CFT deficiencies, jurisdictions
subject to comprehensive sanctions, and known offshore secrecy havens — carry heightened money
laundering risk. Funds moving to or from such jurisdictions warrant additional scrutiny, source
of funds verification, and closer monitoring.

Indicators that warrant enhanced review include payments to or from high-risk or sanctioned
countries, transactions inconsistent with the customer's known business or geographic profile,
and the use of shell companies or nominee intermediaries located in secrecy jurisdictions. Such
activity may require a suspicious activity report.
