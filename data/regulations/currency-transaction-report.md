---
docId: currency-transaction-report
title: Reports of currency transactions exceeding $10,000
citation: 31 CFR 1010.311
source: FinCEN / Bank Secrecy Act
---

Each financial institution must file a Currency Transaction Report (CTR) for each deposit,
withdrawal, exchange of currency, or other payment or transfer, by, through, or to the
institution which involves a transaction in currency of more than $10,000. Multiple currency
transactions are treated as a single transaction if the financial institution has knowledge
that they are by or on behalf of the same person and they result in either cash in or cash
out totaling more than $10,000 during any one business day.

The reporting threshold makes the rapid aggregation of cash, repeated cash deposits just
below $10,000, and unusual transaction velocity meaningful indicators of efforts to evade
the currency reporting requirements. Aggregation of related transactions across a short
window is central to identifying reportable activity.

A financial institution must retain a copy of each report filed for five years from the date
of filing.
